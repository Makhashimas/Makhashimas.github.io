PRIVACY POLICY

Application: Hermes · Operator: Farrel Fayzul Haqqi · Last updated: 23 September 2026

Hermes is a private, single-user personal automation assistant operated by Farrel Fayzul Haqqi. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Google user data Hermes accesses, why, how it is protected, and how you can revoke access at any time.

Summary: Hermes reads bank transaction notification emails from the operator's own Gmail account, records those transactions in a private Google Sheet, and sends the operator a summary. Hermes does not sell, share, or advertise with your data, and its use of Google user data is limited to providing these user-facing features.

1. WHO THIS POLICY COVERS

This policy applies to the Hermes application (the “Application”) and the Google account the operator connects to it. Hermes is used only by its operator; there are no other end users. If you are not the operator of this Application, Hermes does not process your data.

2. WHAT DATA HERMES ACCESSES

When the operator authorizes Hermes through Google's OAuth consent screen, Hermes may access the following Google user data, strictly for the purposes described in Section 3:

Google service / scopeWhat is accessedWhy
Gmail (read-only, modify, send)Messages in the operator's own mailbox, limited to automated bank transaction notifications (e.g. from BNI).Detect new transactions, and email the operator their own scheduled summaries.
Google SheetsOne spreadsheet owned by the operator (“Keuangan BNI”).Append new transaction rows to the operator's personal ledger.
Google DriveFiles created or opened by the Application only.Create and manage the ledger spreadsheet.
Google DocsDocuments created by the Application only.Generate optional written summaries.
Google CalendarCalendar events created by the Application only.Add personal reminders for the operator.
Google Contacts (read-only)Contact names used to label the operator's own messages.Make summaries readable for the operator.

Hermes does not access data from any Google account other than the one the operator explicitly connects.

3. HOW THE DATA IS USED

  • To read automated transaction notifications and extract the amount, time, and merchant.
  • To append those transactions as new rows to the operator's private ledger spreadsheet.
  • To send the operator a periodic summary of their own transactions.

All processing is for the operator's personal use and for the single purpose of providing these features.

4. GOOGLE API SERVICES — LIMITED USE DISCLOSURE

Hermes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • Google user data is not sold, rented, or transferred to any third party.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, ad targeting, or profiling.
  • Google user data is not used to train, fine-tune, or improve any machine learning or artificial intelligence model.
  • Google user data is not read by any human, except where the operator reviews their own data or where required by law.

5. STORAGE, SECURITY & SHARING

Hermes runs locally on the operator's own computer. OAuth credentials are stored locally on that machine and are used only to call Google APIs on the operator's behalf. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Hermes does not operate any server that stores your Google user data, and does not disclose Google user data to any third party, except when strictly required by applicable law.

6. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Transaction data is stored in the operator's own Google Sheet and remains under the operator's control. The operator can delete it at any time directly in Google Sheets.

You can revoke Hermes' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is revoked, Hermes can no longer access your Google user data, and the stored OAuth token is no longer valid.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Hermes is not directed to children and is used solely by its adult operator.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
